Open Visit Bridge

Care that carries on.

Visit Bridge helps a frontline health worker turn an instruction they have already chosen into a reviewed, portable care handoff. Built for World Bank Challenge 4a: Small AI for development, Health.

Try the core flow

  1. Open the app and create your own device-vault passphrase. Use fictional information.
  2. Start a visit and use the built-in sample or type a simple administrative instruction.
  3. Review the original and extracted fields. Confirm unknown or ambiguous details yourself.
  4. Optionally request local AI wording after explicitly installing its approximately 207 MB pack. Compare every word with the source and approve the final patient text. Typed review works without this pack.
  5. Prepare the English card. Record the worker's understanding observation and, with explicit permission, save it on this device.
  6. Open its patient copy to print/download the approved words and marker. Saved cards are encrypted in this browser's vault.
  7. Use Scan care card with that device's marker, or enter its printed reference. Confirm the found wording, open approved replay, and explicitly start marker camera or matching local audio when available.

See the offline and language paths

Wait for Offline access ready before disconnecting. Reload, unlock and reopen a saved card on the same device and origin. Model installation, language packs and speech availability are separate from the app shell.

Spanish demonstrates a constrained return-date-and-clinic template, with its unvalidated translation notice retained. Install its pack before going offline. Camera marker replay follows the expected QR in the camera image; immersive surface-placement AR is an additional mode on supporting devices.

What has been verified

91 automated tests cover approval, structured fields, encrypted storage, local model packs, exact-word playback, QR lookup and replay lifecycles. Desktop browser checks include real local model inference, export/import, saved-card reopening and cached-shell use while its server was stopped.

Physical iPhone tracking, actual airplane mode, speaker pronunciation, qualified Spanish review, print output and measured patient comprehension remain pending. The prototype does not diagnose, prescribe or choose treatment. Marker camera augmentation does not provide persistent world anchoring. No clinical or adherence benefit is claimed.
