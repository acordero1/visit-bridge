# Patient understanding checkpoint

The end-of-visit card now includes a worker-facing repeat-back prompt and three explicit observations: understood, clarified, needs-follow-up. Clarified does not assert comprehension. No patient response, recording, name or automated score is stored. Checking is voluntary: an unmarked saved card states not recorded, and unresolved cards remain saveable. Workers can clear/repeat the check or replay the approved wording. Changing a plan requires the existing source/final wording approvals again.

An observation contains only result, markedAt and approvedStamp. The stamp covers source/wording revisions, handoff revision, language, translation-pack version, source/final approval timestamps and exact final text. Source/field/wording/language edits and fresh approvals clear observations. Validation rejects altered or stale observations. saveStamp also includes the observation so changing/clearing a result requires resaving; earlier saved snapshots remain independent. Schema 5 adds understanding, while schemas 1–4 remain readable as not recorded. IndexedDB remains plaintext until the next privacy checkpoint.

Patient layout preserves every approved character and line order. Symbols distinguish the confirmed structured action, place, date, time, item and additional task. Unknown/manual lines use a neutral document symbol; the app does not split sentences, infer actions or invent clinical imagery. Icons supplement the text and are hidden from accessibility APIs. Large text, wrapping, minimum 48px result controls, pressed-state labels, live result text and focus handling support accessible interaction. A real screen-reader session and participant interpretation of symbols remain unverified.

Home separately reports cached shell, English typing, local English dictation, optional local AI files, the constrained Spanish text pack and browser-reported matching local voices. Input language is explicitly English in Capture. Availability checks do not start microphones, speech playback or model downloads. Browser voice reporting does not prove speaker quality or physical-device offline behavior. Spanish is output only for the exact return/date/clinic template, not general translation or Spanish dictation.

The updated optional Spanish JSON is version 1.1.0, separately cached and SHA-256 verified, with an unvalidated Spanish repeat-back prompt. Older 1.0.0 pack snapshots remain readable; their unchanged approved words are retained. Patient answer content is never collected. Professional/community language validation is still absent.
